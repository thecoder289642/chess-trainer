# Opening Trainer

Learn and practise chess openings against the moves real players make, personalised from your own games.

**Use it:** https://thecoder289642.github.io/chess-trainer/

1. Open the link and enter your **chess.com** and/or **Lichess** username, then press **Load my games**. Your games are downloaded into your browser only.
2. **Overview** shows which openings you face, how you score against each, what to learn next, your weak spots and lines due for review.
3. **Learn** teaches each opening's main line plus the deviations you'll meet (arrows first, then practise from memory).
4. **Play** lets you play out openings against popular moves from Lichess players at your rating, Masters games, your own games, or your repertoire.
5. Optional: for live Lichess statistics, create a free token with no permissions ticked at lichess.org/account/oauth/token and paste it in Settings (⚙).

Install it like an app: on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android/Chrome use "Install app".

Developers: see `CLAUDE.md`. `npm install && npm run build`.
